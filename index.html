    How the colony works

    1. Hold, hatch. Every wallet holding the token hatches an ant. The bigger your bag, the bigger your ant — from tiny workers to soldiers, majors and the Queen.
    2. Fees become sugar. This coin launched as a pump.fun Holder Rewards coin: the creator fee from every trade goes to holders, not to the dev. It collects in the coin's on-chain vault, the sugar pile in the middle.
    3. Ants haul it home. Every so often pump.fun splits the vault between every wallet holding at least ~$20 of the coin, pro-rata to their bag. Your ant carries your share back to its mound.
    4. Paid in SOL. It lands in your wallet automatically, no claiming and no staking. Every payout on this page is read straight from the chain.
    • Queen the #1 holder
    • Major 1%+ of supply
    • Soldier 0.1%+
    • Worker everyone else
